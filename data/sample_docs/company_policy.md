# Nimbus Analytics — Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

## Working hours
The standard working week at Nimbus Analytics is 40 hours, Sunday to Thursday, with core hours from 10:00 to 15:00. Employees may start any time between 07:00 and 10:00.

## Remote work
Employees can work remotely up to two days per week after completing their three-month probation period. Remote days must be registered in the HR portal by Thursday of the previous week. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department director.

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 21 days of paid annual leave per year. After five years of service the allowance increases to 30 days. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year and must be used before the end of March.

## Sick leave
Employees must notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of sickness. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than two consecutive days.

## Equipment
Each employee receives a laptop, a monitor and a headset. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to IT support within 24 hours. The IT help desk can be reached at extension 4040.

## Training budget
Every employee has an annual training budget of 1,500 USD for courses, certifications and conferences. Requests must be approved by the direct manager at least two weeks in advance.
